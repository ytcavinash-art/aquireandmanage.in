Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to key questions from residents, tenants, developers, and redevelopment stakeholders across Mumbai.

An SRA (Slum Rehabilitation Authority) project in Mumbai is a statutory redevelopment scheme designed to replace informal slum settlements with modern multi-storey residential towers, granting free permanent rehabilitation tenements to eligible slum dwellers while enabling planned urban development.
Eligibility depends on official government cutoff dates (such as 01/01/2000 or 01/01/2011) based on verified proof of occupancy. The Slum Rehabilitation Authority publishes an official Annexure II list certifying eligible hutment dwellers.
Key required documents include Ration Card, Voter ID (Electoral Roll extract), Electricity Bills, Aadhaar Card, Photo Pass, and property tax receipts proving continuous residence prior to the applicable cutoff date.
Developers provide monthly transit rent allowances or alternative transit accommodation along with one-time shifting charges to eligible residents until possession of their permanent rehabilitation unit is handed over.
Yes, A&M Advisory specializes in statutory liaisoning, Annexure II processing, Letter of Intent (LOI), Intimation of Disapproval (IOD), Commencement Certificate (CC), and NOC clearances across SRA, MHADA, and BMC.
A&M Advisory sets up dedicated on-ground community information centers (IEC booths), helpline numbers, and resident grievance resolution support to address all resident queries transparently.
Annexure II is an official statutory document issued by the Competent Authority certifying verified eligible slum dwellers, their structure numbers, carpet area, and entitlement status. It serves as the legal foundation for rehabilitation apartment allocation.
Under current SRA guidelines in Maharashtra, a minimum of 51% consent from eligible slum dwellers is required to initiate a slum rehabilitation scheme and officially appoint a developer.
A&M Advisory manages door-to-door household surveys, document collection and verification, consensus building, monthly transit rent disbursement tracking, lottery allotment coordination, and final unit possession handover.
We bridge communication between developers, housing societies, slum dwellers, and government authorities using structured IEC activities, legal compliance audits, and proactive grievance resolution to prevent litigation and construction halts.
