MUMBAI SRA GUIDANCE

Slum Rehabilitation FAQs in Mumbai

Answers to common questions from residents, tenants, developers, and redevelopment stakeholders in Mumbai.

An SRA (Slum Rehabilitation Authority) project in Mumbai is a statutory redevelopment scheme designed to replace informal slum settlements with modern multi-storey residential towers, granting free permanent rehabilitation tenements to eligible slum dwellers while enabling planned urban development.
Eligibility depends on official government cutoff dates (such as 01/01/2000 or 01/01/2011) based on verified proof of occupancy. The Slum Rehabilitation Authority publishes an official Annexure II list certifying eligible hutment dwellers.
Key required documents include Ration Card, Voter ID (Electoral Roll extract), Electricity Bills, Aadhaar Card, Photo Pass, and property tax receipts proving continuous residence prior to the applicable cutoff date.
Developers provide monthly transit rent allowances or alternative transit accommodation along with one-time shifting charges to eligible residents until possession of their permanent rehabilitation unit is handed over.
